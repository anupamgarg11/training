Do not change your foot strike. Which part of the foot lands first is not the problem, and forcing it is how calves and Achilles tear. What matters is landing UNDER yourself instead of reaching out in front — and cadence is the only lever that moves that. Cadence DOES rise with speed — measured inside the segments on 29 Sep and 1 Oct: 146 easy, ~160 during 1 km reps, 173 on the fastest stride. It drives the other two, costs nothing, and fewer, longer steps load the shin that has now flared four times - the last one on an easy run, which cadence alone does not explain.

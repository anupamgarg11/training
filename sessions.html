Progression

Hit the TOP of the rep range on every set → add 2.5 kg next session and drop back to the bottom.

Coarse increments

If the prescribed weight won't let you hit the rep range, the rep range wins. On machines that jump 5 kg, progress by reps → then a 3-second lowering → then an extra set.

Everything listed gets done

There is no optional tier. If a session has to be shortened, drop SETS across the board rather than dropping exercises — you lose less that way.

Order matters

Whatever goes first gets your best sets. Shoulder press proved that the hard way.

Every exercise listed gets done. Short on time? Drop sets across the board, not exercises. This page is the single source of truth for loads.

Progression

Hit the TOP of the rep range on every set → add 2.5 kg next session and drop back to the bottom.

Coarse increments

If the prescribed weight won't let you hit the rep range, the rep range wins. On machines that jump 5 kg, progress by reps → then a 3-second lowering → then an extra set.

Never run the day after leg day in heavy shoes

3 Sep: shin pain on a run 33h after a 26-set leg day (tibialis 3x30 + 5 sets of calf) in the Ultraboost. Two loads on one muscle. Race-day shoes are the Evo SL — train in them.

Calluses

File them flat weekly with a pumice stone after a shower — smooth, not gone. Moisturise at NIGHT, never before training. Most rips come from gripping too deep: let the bar sit low, at the crease where fingers meet palm, not mid-palm where the skin bunches and shears. Skip gloves — they increase effective bar thickness and make grip worse. If one tears: trim it flush, cover it, and use straps to keep pulling while it heals.

Grip, not lats, is capping your pull day

Chalk up for everything (liquid chalk — allowed where loose chalk is not). STRAPS on the last 1-2 sets of pulldowns and rows only, never on pull-ups. Dead hangs at the end fix the root cause. If a back set ends because your hands opened, it did not train your back.

When the next plate is a big jump, earn it with reps

Plate-stack machines step 7 kg (shoulder press, rows, rear delt, hip abduction, chest press): 36/43/50, 52/59. Cable stacks step 5 kg. Dumbbells 5 kg total. From 43 on a plate stack that is a 16% jump, and forcing it wrecks form. Protocol: fill the rep range on EVERY set, then add 2-3 reps beyond the top on every set, THEN take the jump and expect to fall back to the bottom of the range. Dumbbells jump 5 kg total (~17%) — same rule, or use a top set with back-off sets.

Everything listed gets done

There is no optional tier. If a session has to be shortened, drop SETS across the board rather than dropping exercises — you lose less that way.

Order matters

Whatever goes first gets your best sets. Shoulder press proved that the hard way.
